# Lesson review: Day 1 (Me) and Day 2 (Order and ask)

Reviewed 20 Nov 2026 by Claude for the owner, in place of the T7 checklist. Source of truth: `w2s-site/sprint_content.py`
(sentences, Fill the Gap, Sound Lab, Quick Answers, Course, clips), `w2s-site/apply_sprint.py` (Meet someone new) and
`w2s-show/showcase.pre-sprint.html` (Café order). **Mei should still listen to the recorded voices and read this once
before the changes go live.**

## Verdict

Both days are pitched right for an A1 adult who studied some English at school but freezes when speaking. Recognition
work (Word Order, Fill the Gap) is easy on purpose, and the speaking tasks carry the stretch: five true sentences without
reading (Day 1), and a full café order (Day 2). I fixed the problems that made the lessons confusing or wrong. I did
not make them harder: what was missing was consistency, not difficulty.

## Open points (not changed; Mei to decide)

1. **Day 1 Sound Lab uses rare words** (espy, squire, esquire, estrange). The pairs are right for hearing the extra "e",
   but a learner does not know the words. Option: keep the pairs, or switch to a "which one is English?" round with
   school/eschool, Spain/Espain (needs a game change).
2. **Currency:** Day 2 uses pounds ("That's four pounds fifty"). Right if most buyers are in the UK or travelling there;
   for a Latin American audience dollars may feel closer.
3. **Fill the Gap, Day 1:** "She ___ coffee." (likes) tests third-person -s, which Day 1 does not teach. Keep it as the
   one stretch item, or swap for "I ___ in a hospital." (work / am work / working), the "I am work" mistake.
4. The recorded voices were checked by length and order, not by ear. Listen to five or six clips on a phone before
   going live (the Sound Lab single words first: "state", "hat", "and").
