## Principles

1. **Speak from Day 1.** Each day ends with a spoken task, not a quiz. [E: Sprint DAYS goals] Producing language shows
   learners the gaps in what they know: Merrill Swain's output hypothesis, already cited on the "Cómo enseñamos" page. [E: page copy]
2. **Mistakes come from Spanish, so they are predictable.** Each lesson names one transfer mistake ("I have thirty years",
   "I have hunger") and the fix. [E: COURSE in sprint_content.py] [A: the 16 listed mistakes are the most frequent ones;
   Mei to confirm from her teaching.]
3. **Recognise, then build, then say.** Word Order and Fill the Gap (recognise) → Flash Translate (build) → Quick Answers
   and the scene (say). [E: game order]
4. **Few phrases, used many times.** 5 key phrases a lesson, 8 sentences a day in the games. [E]
5. **Same model voice every time.** Recorded US voices for Days 1-2. [E]
6. **Hints step down.** Starter → word bank → full model sentence, so a learner is never stuck. [E: scene hints]

## Difficulty rules

- Recognition is easy on purpose; speaking carries the stretch. [E: lesson review 20 Nov]
