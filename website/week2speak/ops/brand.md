## Who we are

- Week2Speak is a speaking-practice site for Spanish-speaking adults who freeze in English. [E: site copy, MARKETING_PLAN.md §1]
- Mei is the human face: an English tutor who speaks Spanish, Portuguese and English. [E: about page]
- Spanish first at week2speak.com/, English at /en/. [E: site]

## Promise

- "Tu primera conversación en inglés, en 7 días": a goal, never a guarantee of fluency. [E: MARKETING_PLAN.md "What we never say"]
- Small, real, out loud: about 20 minutes a day, real sentences, Day 1 and Day 7 recorded. [E: Sprint page]

## Voice (how we write)

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Short sentences, tú, neutral Latin American Spanish | Slang from one country; vosotros |
| Name the mistake and why Spanish causes it | Shame ("está mal", "error grave") |
| Encourage effort ("ya has empezado") | Promise results, cures or speed |
| Show real numbers (5 places while true) | Countdown timers, "solo hoy", fake scarcity |

[A: neutral Latin American Spanish suits most buyers. The only two accounts so far are in the UK; check again at 20.]

## Look and sound

- Two Language Buddies guide practice: **May** (warm and playful) and **Kairo** (laid-back and confident). Ruby and
  Max are retired. Mei is the real person; a Buddy is never presented as a human. [E: buddy_roster.py]
- Recorded voices chosen by the owner, one set per accent: American (Brittney, Joe; Genevieve for May, Young Jamal
  for Kairo), British (Charlotte, Marcus Black), Australian (Jane, Paul). [E: audio_build.py `VOICES`]
- The site never fakes an accent: if a line has no recording and the device has no voice for an accent, that
  accent is not offered. [E: deployed 20 Nov]
- Privacy is part of the brand: no trackers, no requests to other domains. [E: privacy notice]

## Never

- Fake reviews, staged learners, testimonials without written consent. [E: MARKETING_PLAN.md §7]
- Disparaging other apps or teachers; medical claims about anxiety. [E: MARKETING_PLAN.md]
